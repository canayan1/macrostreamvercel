Which brokers does it work with?

Any broker that gives you a MetaTrader 5 report. MT4 reports are not supported yet.

Do prop limits show a real breach?

Not exactly. A history report has no intraday equity, so the page measures closed-trade balance only. A real breach may have come earlier.

Is this advice?

No. The page describes your past trades. It does not tell you what to do.
