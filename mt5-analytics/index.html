Kalibre Trade Analytics: see where you make and lose money

Drop in your MT5 history report. See your results by symbol, hour, weekday, session, trade size and the trade after a loss. On a prop account, check how close each day came to the loss limits. The file is read in your browser. In beta.

Try it with sample dataGet early access

How it works

  1. Export your reportIn MT5, right-click the History tab and choose Report, then HTML.
  2. Drop it inThe file is read in your browser. No install, no account.
  3. Read the breakdownsOverview, segments, prop limits and views you save.

Features

  • MT5 report, your own spreadsheet and Kalibre Bridge In beta
    MT5 HTML and Open XML reports, Excel and CSV with the template or column mapping, and 90 days of trade history from the Bridge.
  • Segments and breakdowns In beta
    Symbol, open hour, weekday, session, direction, holding time, trade size, previous trade result and losing streak.
  • Prop limits In beta
    Daily and max floors from closed-trade balance. A history report has no intraday equity.
  • Saved views In beta
    Views are stored in this browser.
  • Replay Planned
    Step through your own MT5 history candle by candle.
  • Weekly report Planned
    Your biggest losing segments and limit proximity, every week.

What happens to your data

  • The report or spreadsheet file is read in your browser and is not sent to Kalibre servers.
  • Only the account currency is read from the report header. Your account number and name are not read or stored.
  • Saved views stay in this browser and do not contain your trades.
  • If you use Kalibre Bridge, data travels from MT5 to your browser encrypted. Details are on the security page.

Import your own trade log

If you keep your trades in Excel or another spreadsheet, download the template, write one closed trade per row and drop the file into Trade Analytics. The same analysis runs on it.

  • Required columns: open_time, close_time, symbol, side (buy or sell), volume, open_price, close_price, profit (before commission and swap).
  • Optional: commission, swap, stop_loss, take_profit, utc_offset, account_currency, ticket, row_type (deposit, withdrawal), strategy, tag, note.
  • Every row that cannot be read is listed with its row number and the reason. Formulas are not run.

Questions

  • Which brokers does it work with? Any broker whose MetaTrader 5 terminal exports reports. MT4 reports are not supported yet.
  • Do the prop limits show a real breach? Not exactly. A history report has no intraday loss on open positions, so the page measures closed-trade balance only. A real breach may have happened earlier.
  • Is this advice? No. The page describes your past trades. It does not tell you what to do.
