MT5 account analytics and backtesting in your browser

Connect your MetaTrader 5 account read-only, see your balance, positions and closed trades in one dashboard, and test your own rules on your own MT5 price history. It is in early access.

What it does

  • Account dashboard In beta
    Balance, equity, open positions, the symbols you follow and the last 90 days of closed trades.
  • Backtest on your MT5 data In beta
    Build a rule with blocks or a form and run Quick Backtest on candles from your terminal.
  • Overfitting checks In beta
    Training and test periods, probability of backtest overfitting (PBO), deflated Sharpe ratio and walk-forward analysis.
  • Replay Planned
    Step through your own MT5 history candle by candle and practise entries.
  • Trade review Planned
    Where you lose money, by symbol, hour and setup.

How it connects

The Kalibre Bridge add-on runs in your MT5 terminal and sends your account data to your browser, encrypted. It is read-only and places no orders. You don't give us your MT5 password. Details are on the security page.

Questions

  • Which brokers does it work with? Any broker that offers MetaTrader 5. MT4 is not supported.
  • Does it place trades on my account? No. Kalibre Bridge only reads.
  • Where is my data processed? The dashboard and backtests run in your browser. Our server holds only encrypted packets, for at most 2 hours.
