Does my firm allow EAs?

Check your firm's rules on EAs; some firms restrict them. Rule Guard runs as an Expert Advisor in MT5.

Can it stop trades I open by hand?

No. It counts positions opened by hand or by another EA when it works out the remaining room, but it cannot block those orders.

Does it guarantee that I pass?

No. It watches the loss limits only. Slippage and price gaps can cause losses beyond a stop.
