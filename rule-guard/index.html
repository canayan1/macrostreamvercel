Kalibre Rule Guard: stay inside your prop firm's loss limits on MT5

Rule Guard tracks your firm's daily and total loss limits on your MT5 account, stops new trades near the floor and sends you a weekly report of rule breaks and costly mistakes. It is in early access.

Get early accessCheck your account

What it does

  • Firm presets In testing on demo
    FTMO 2-Step, FTMO 1-Step, FTMO Instant and The5ers High Stakes. FundedNext and FundingPips Planned
  • Floors calculated the way your firm does In testing on demo
    The daily floor uses the start-of-day balance. The total floor uses the starting balance or the peak. The day resets at the firm's time.
  • No new trades near the floor In testing on demo
    At twice the safety margin, Kalibre Engine opens no new trades. At one safety margin, it closes its own positions.
  • Challenge dashboard Planned
    Distance to the daily and total floor, progress to the profit target and trading days, in your browser.
  • Weekly rule and mistake report Planned
    Which trades broke a rule or came close, losses after days near the floor, oversized positions.

How it connects

Rule Guard runs as an Expert Advisor in your MT5 terminal. For the dashboard and report, the Kalibre Bridge add-on sends your account data to your browser, encrypted. You don't give us your MT5 password. Details are on the security page.

To check past days against your firm's limits now, drop your MT5 report into Trade Analytics.

Questions

  • Does my firm allow EAs? Check your firm's rules on EAs; some firms restrict them. Rule Guard runs as an Expert Advisor in MT5.
  • Can it stop trades I open by hand? No. It counts positions opened by hand or by another EA when it calculates the room left, but it cannot block those orders.
  • Does it guarantee I pass? No. It only tracks loss limits. Slippage and gaps can cause losses beyond the stop.
  • Is Kalibre a prop firm? No. Kalibre is not affiliated with any prop firm. Firm names are used only to describe their rules.
