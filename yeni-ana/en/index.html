Test your own strategy on your own data. Risk first.

Kalibre Lab lets you build rules on your broker account's data, test them with costs included and see whether a result is robust or luck. Your data stays with you: MetaTrader 5 data arrives encrypted, and your Alpaca and Tradier keys stay in your browser. You make the decisions; we never sell signals.

      Modules

      Dashboard
      Your account, quotes, open positions and closed trades; profit factor, win rate, realised drawdown.
      Strategy builder
      Recipe-like blocks in simple mode, the full form in advanced mode; both edit the same rule.
      Quick test
      Runs the rule on your own candles in seconds; add spread, commission and slippage.
      Optimisation
      Training and test periods, neighbouring settings, a luck adjustment and a prop-rule filter.
      Prop shield
      Watches daily and total loss limits on every tick and sizes lots to the limit.
      Volatility lab
      See when volatility clusters with EWMA, Yang-Zhang and GARCH.

      What our server sees, and what it cannot

      MetaTrader 5 data leaves your computer encrypted and opens only in your browser. Details are on the security page. Security

      What our server sees

      • Unreadable, encrypted packets
      • Packet size and arrival time
      • A random-looking channel number

      What our server cannot see

      • Which symbols you follow, and the prices
      • Your positions, balance, profit and loss
      • Your account number and password
      • Your pairing code, Alpaca and Tradier keys
