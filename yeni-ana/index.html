      Features

      Dashboard
      Account, quotes, open positions and closed trades, with profit factor, win rate and realized drawdown.
      Strategy Builder
      Blocks in Simple mode and a full form in Advanced mode. Both edit the same rule.
      Quick Backtest
      Runs a rule on your candles in seconds. Add spread, commission and slippage.
      Optimization
      Training and test periods, neighboring settings, deflated Sharpe ratio and a prop rule filter.
      Prop Shield
      Checks daily and total loss limits on every tick and sizes lots to the remaining room.
      Volatility Lab
      EWMA, Yang-Zhang and GARCH volatility models.

      Security

      Kalibre Bridge encrypts MT5 data with AES-256 before sending it. Only your browser can decrypt it. Read the security details

      What our relay server sees

      • Encrypted packets
      • Packet size and arrival time
      • A channel ID derived from your pairing code

      What it cannot see

      • Symbols and prices
      • Positions, balance and trade history
      • Your pairing code and your Alpaca or Tradier keys
      • Your MT5 password, which is never requested
