This notice summarises the cookies and similar browser storage used on kalibremarkets.com and in Kalibre Lab. More detail on how we use personal data is in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## In short {#short}

- We use only what is needed to run the Site, plus Google Analytics **if you accept it**.
- We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
- Declining is as easy as accepting, and you can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link in the footer or on the Privacy Policy page.

## 1. Strictly necessary {#necessary}

These are needed for the Site to work and do not require consent.

| Name or type | Set by | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Security check cookie | Vercel (our host) | Protects the Site against bots and attacks | Short-lived, set by Vercel |
| Sign-in session (browser local storage) | Kalibre Markets via Supabase | Keeps you signed in to your account | Until you sign out or it expires |
| `kalibre-cerez-rizasi` (local storage) | Kalibre Markets | Remembers your cookie choice | Until you clear site data |

## 2. Analytics (only with your consent) {#analytics}

| Name | Set by | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Google Analytics | Counts visits and shows how pages are used | Up to 2 years |

Google Analytics is not loaded until you click "Accept". If you later withdraw consent, we stop loading it and remove its cookies from your browser.

## 3. Local storage used by Kalibre Lab {#local-storage}

Kalibre Lab stores your work in your browser's local storage so it works without an account: strategy drafts, journal entries, test results, interface preferences, your Kalibre Bridge pairing code and any broker connection keys you enter. These are part of the service you ask for, stay on your device and are not sent to us (except items you choose to save to your account while signed in). Clearing your browser's site data deletes them permanently.

## 4. Third-party pages {#third-party}

When paid plans open and you pay, you are taken to Stripe's checkout page, and when you sign in with Google, to Google's sign-in page. Those pages set their own cookies under Stripe's and Google's policies. Fonts may be loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address but does not set cookies for this purpose.
