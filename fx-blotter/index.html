Export. Drop. Read.

  1. Export the blotter

    From 360T, Murex, Bloomberg or the Excel your desk keeps.

  2. Drop it in

    Columns are matched for you. The file stays in your browser.

  3. Read the day

    P&L by price and points, positions, and seven checks.

Built around how a treasury desk reads its day.

Price and points, apart.

Realized P&L is split into spot price and forward points. Carry shows as overnight swap and MTM covers the open position. Swaps count as rolls and do not change the net position.

Every total opens its deals.

Group by pair, book, dealer, item type, deal size, hour or weekday. Click any figure and the deal rows behind it open.

Positions by value date.

A ladder from today through spot, one week, one month and beyond a year, per currency, next to your own limits.

Reads the file as it is.

Report title rows, total rows, headers in English, Turkish, German or French and local number formats are handled. Each column match shows its confidence, and you can change it.

Seven checks on every file.

Row counts, subtotals against the grand total, amount times rate on every leg, notionals by currency, the blotter's local-currency column, open lots against positions, and FIFO against average cost. A failed check turns red.

Twelve hand-computed cases match exactly.

Your data stays with you

The blotter is read in the browser on your machine. Counterparty names are dropped by default.

Bring your own rate files

Valuation uses the rates you already have. We supply no market data, so there is no new licence to sign.

Which systems can it read?

CSV and Excel exports from systems such as 360T, FXall, Bloomberg FXGO and Murex, and blotters kept by hand in Excel.

Which products are covered?

Spot, outright forwards, swap legs and NDFs. Options are detected and left out with a reason.

Is opportunity cost shown?

Not yet. It will be added once its definition is agreed with desks.
