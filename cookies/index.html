This notice summarises the cookies and similar browser storage used on kalibremarkets.com and in Kalibre Lab. More detail on how we use personal data is in our Privacy Policy.

In short

  • We use only what is needed to run the Site, plus Google Analytics if you accept it.
  • We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • Declining is as easy as accepting, and you can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link in the footer or on the Privacy Policy page.

3. Local storage used by Kalibre Lab

Kalibre Lab stores your work in your browser's local storage so it works without an account: strategy drafts, journal entries, test results, interface preferences, your Kalibre Bridge pairing code and any broker connection keys you enter. These are part of the service you ask for, stay on your device and are not sent to us (except items you choose to save to your account while signed in). Clearing your browser's site data deletes them permanently.

4. Third-party pages

When paid plans open and you pay, you are taken to Stripe's checkout page, and when you sign in with Google, to Google's sign-in page. Those pages set their own cookies under Stripe's and Google's policies. Fonts may be loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address but does not set cookies for this purpose.

5. Managing cookies

Use the "Cookie settings" link to change your choice.

You can also block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings; if you block necessary items, signing in and parts of Kalibre Lab may not work.
