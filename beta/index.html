Beta programme guide

This guide covers what is in the Kalibre Lab beta, a first session, what to avoid and how to send feedback.

Use a demo account in the beta programme. Do not connect an account that trades real money with an invited beta license. All results are simulations and may not include costs; do not make real-money decisions with them. Details are in the risk disclosure.

How do I get my beta license?

Your invitation includes a beta license key. Sign in to your Kalibre Markets account (it is created when you first sign in) and use "Link to my account" on the Account page. The key then appears under Products and licenses. No payment details are needed.

How long does a beta license last? A beta license is free and personal. It covers Kalibre Bridge only and expires on the date shown on your Account page. Subscriptions are not open during the beta.

What is in the beta?

  • MetaTrader 5 bridge and Dashboard: your demo MT5 balance, open positions, the symbols you follow and the last 90 days of closed trades, end-to-end encrypted.
  • Strategy builder: Simple mode (blocks) and Advanced mode (form); both edit the same strategy.
  • Quick Backtest: runs a rule on your candles in seconds. Costs are optional and zero by default.
  • Optimization: parameter scan, training and test periods, heat map, a measure of whether a result could be luck.
  • Alpaca (paper) and Tradier (sandbox): direct browser connection to US brokers.
Automated trading is not part of the beta programme. Kalibre Engine (the add-on that runs a rule file in MT5) and the prop shield are not included in the beta license; your beta license covers Kalibre Bridge (read-only) only. No orders are placed on any account in the beta programme.

First session (about 30 minutes)

  1. Connect your demo MT5 account on the connect page: download the add-on, paste the code, add the permission address, press OK. Note any step where you get stuck.
  2. Open the Dashboard: are balance, quotes and positions right? Compare with what MT5 shows.
  3. In the strategy builder, build a strategy in Simple mode (e.g. a moving-average cross) or load an educational example; fill in the risk section yourself; run Quick Backtest.
  4. On the Optimization page, scan two parameters; look at the heat map and the test-period result.
  5. If you like, also connect an Alpaca paper or Tradier sandbox account on the connect page and try the Dashboard with it.

Please don't

  • Connect a real account.
  • Run rule files for automated trading on any account; there is no automated trading in the beta.
  • Share your pairing code, license key or Alpaca/Tradier keys with anyone, including us.
  • Use simulation results for investment decisions.

What feedback do we want?

  • Setup friction: which step, and what the screen said.
  • Wrong numbers: compared with MT5 or your broker; with a screenshot if possible (cover your account number).
  • Confusing wording: which sentence, and what you understood.
  • Crashes or freezes: browser, operating system and the steps you took.

What do we see about you?

We cannot see your market data, positions or trades. Kalibre Bridge data is encrypted and decrypted only in your browser. Your browser requests Alpaca and Tradier data directly. What we do see: your Kalibre Markets account e-mail and profile, the strategy drafts you save while signed in, the feedback you send us and the MT5 account number your beta license is used on (to bind the license to the account).

Send feedback

Fill in the form or send it by email (a template opens).

Thank you, your feedback reached us.

Do not include pairing codes, license keys, API keys or passwords. If you want to send a screenshot, cover your account number and balance and email it.

The form is delivered to us through the FormSubmit service and is used only to fix the product. Details are in the privacy policy.
