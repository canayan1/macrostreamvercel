What is inside.

Trade Analytics.

Drop the MT5 history report. Results by symbol, hour, weekday, session, size and the trade after a loss; your days closest to a prop limit; an account review to keep as PDF or share as a link. Ask it a question in plain words and get the table.

About Trade Analytics

Rule Guard.

An MT5 add-on that watches the firm's daily and total loss limits every second. A warning at twice the safety margin, a lock at one, alerts on screen and on your phone. It never opens a trade.

About Rule Guard

Desk Signal.

Colour signals from one screen to another in one key. Urgent ones ring until answered, and every signal is logged with the time it was seen and done.

About Desk Signal
