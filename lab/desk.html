Signal the desk in one key.

Pick a signal, pick who gets it. The orb in the corner of their screen lights up in that colour. Urgent signals keep ringing until someone answers, and you see who has seen it and who is done.

Pick a signal first.

Keys: 1…5 pick a signal, then a person or group key sends it. Enter repeats the last send, Esc clears. The desktop app takes the same keys from any window with ⌃⌥.

Who has answered

Urgent signals grow on the trader's screen after 10 seconds without an answer. After 20 seconds the row turns red here, so you know who to call.

Signal log

Every signal is logged with the time it was sent, seen and done. Send one to start.

This demo runs in your browser and sends nothing to a server. Signals carry a fixed meaning from your board, never free text and never orders.
