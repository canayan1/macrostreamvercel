6. Kalibre Bridge

6.1 Read-only. Kalibre Bridge reads the price history of symbols you select, your open positions and a summary of your account from your MetaTrader 5 terminal, encrypts that data on your computer and sends it to Kalibre Lab in your browser. It does not place, change or close orders, and it does not change your account settings.

6.2 No passwords. Kalibre Bridge never asks for, reads or sends your MetaTrader 5 password or your broker login password.

6.3 End-to-end encryption. Data is encrypted on your computer with keys derived from a pairing code that Kalibre Lab creates in your browser. We never receive the pairing code, so we cannot read the data and cannot recover a lost code. Our relay only passes the encrypted packets along and deletes each one automatically within two hours.

6.4 Pairing code. Keep your pairing code private. If you think someone else has it, create a new code in Kalibre Lab and enter it in the add-on.

6.5 Licence check. Like Kalibre Engine, Kalibre Bridge checks your licence with our server when it starts and about every six hours. That check sends your licence key and your MetaTrader 5 account number. See the Privacy Policy.

6.6 Not a monitoring system. Kalibre Bridge depends on your terminal being open, your internet connection, your broker's servers and our relay. It may miss or delay data. Do not rely on it to monitor open positions; use your broker's platform for that.
