Beta programme guide

Thank you for trying Kalibre Lab. This page explains what is in the beta, what to do in your first session, what to avoid and how to send feedback.

Use a demo account in the beta programme. Do not connect an account that trades real money with an invited beta licence. All results are simulations and may not include costs; do not make real-money decisions with them. Details are in the risk disclosure.

How do I get my beta licence?

Your invitation comes with an invite code. On the Pricing page, accept the terms and press "Subscribe"; on the checkout page choose "Add promotion code" and enter your code. The code makes the first 3 months free and no card is needed. After checkout your licence key appears on the License page. If you were sent a licence key directly, save it on the same page.

What happens after 3 months? When the free period ends, the subscription moves to the paid monthly plan and Stripe asks you for payment. If you do not want to continue, cancel on the License page before the period ends; if no payment is made the licence closes within a few days. A licence opened with an invite code covers Kalibre Bridge only.

What is in the beta?

  • MetaTrader 5 bridge and Dashboard: your demo MT5 balance, open positions, the symbols you follow and the last 90 days of closed trades, end-to-end encrypted.
  • Strategy builder: Simple mode (blocks) and Advanced mode (form); both edit the same strategy.
  • Quick test: tries a rule on your own candles in seconds. It does not include costs.
  • Optimization: parameter scan, training and test periods, heat map, a measure of whether a result could be luck.
  • Alpaca (paper) and Tradier (sandbox): direct browser connection to US brokers.
Automated trading is not part of the beta programme. Kalibre Engine (the add-on that runs a rule file in MT5) and the prop shield are not included in the beta licence; your beta licence covers Kalibre Bridge (read-only) only. No orders are placed on any account in the beta programme.

First session (about 30 minutes)

  1. Connect your demo MT5 account on the connect page: download the add-on, paste the code, add the permission address, press OK. Note any step where you get stuck.
  2. Open the Dashboard: are balance, quotes and positions right? Compare with what MT5 shows.
  3. In the strategy builder, build a strategy in Simple mode (e.g. a moving-average cross) or load an educational example; fill in the risk section yourself; run the quick test.
  4. On the Optimization page, scan two parameters; look at the heat map and the test-period result.
  5. If you like, also connect an Alpaca paper or Tradier sandbox account on the connect page and try the Dashboard with it.

Please don't

  • Connect a real account.
  • Run rule files for automated trading on any account; there is no automated trading in the beta.
  • Share your pairing code, licence key or Alpaca/Tradier keys with anyone, including us.
  • Use simulation results for investment decisions.

What feedback do we want?

  • Setup friction: which step, and what the screen said.
  • Wrong numbers: compared with MT5 or your broker; with a screenshot if possible (cover your account number).
  • Confusing wording: which sentence, and what you understood.
  • Crashes or freezes: browser, operating system and the steps you took.

What do we see about you?

We cannot see your market data, positions or trades: data from the bridge is encrypted and opens only in your browser; Alpaca and Tradier data comes straight to your browser. All we see is the feedback you send us and the MT5 account number your beta licence is used on (to bind the licence to the account).

Send feedback

Fill in the form or send it by email (a template opens).

Thank you, your feedback reached us.

Do not include pairing codes, licence keys, API keys or passwords. If you want to send a screenshot, cover your account number and balance and email it.

The form is delivered to us through the FormSubmit service and is used only to fix the product. Details are in the privacy policy.
