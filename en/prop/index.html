Don't fail the challenge on a rule breach.

Most prop firm challenges are not lost to a bad strategy but to one bad day: the daily loss or total drawdown limit is breached and the account is closed. The Kalibre Engine shield watches those limits on every tick and steps in before you notice.

See pricingCheck your own account

What the shield does

  • It tracks both floors. The daily floor comes from the start-of-day balance, the total floor from the starting or peak balance; whichever is higher applies. The day resets at the firm's time (for FTMO, 00:00 Central European time, including summer time).
  • It sizes lots to the limit. A new trade's loss at its stop, together with the risk left to the stops of open positions, cannot exceed the distance to the floor. Positions you opened by hand on the account count too.
  • It stops first, then closes. Once the distance to the floor is down to twice the safety margin, no new trades are opened. At one margin, Engine closes its own positions and trades no more until the day resets.
  • It applies the news rule. If the firm bans trading around high-impact news, no new trades are opened in that window.

Scope and limits

  • The shield is part of Kalibre Engine. Engine runs the rule file you build in Kalibre Lab on your own account; we sell no signals and make no decisions for you.
  • Kalibre is not a prop firm and is not affiliated with any firm. Firm names appear only to describe their rules.
  • Slippage and gaps can cause losses beyond the stop; that is what the safety margin is for.
