About

Kalibre builds software for people who want to test a strategy honestly before putting money behind it.

Why?

The tools retail traders have usually show return and hide risk. On a bank's treasury desk the first question is not what a position could earn, but what it could lose on its worst day. An economist, likewise, looks less at one period's result and more at whether the conditions that produced it will repeat.

Kalibre Lab brings that way of looking at things to your own data: write the assumption down, test it on your broker's prices, see risk on the same line as return.

How we work

  • The data is yours. We publish no prices. The lab runs on your MT5 account's data or on clearly labelled SYNTHETIC samples.
  • The key is yours. We cannot read data passing through the bridge. That is guaranteed by the protocol itself, not by a promise (security).
  • The method is open. We write down how a number is calculated and what it leaves out (methodology).
  • We give no advice. You decide which rule to build and when to trade.
