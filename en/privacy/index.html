Privacy Policy and Information Notice

Effective date: 21 October 2026

This is a translation. If the English and Turkish versions differ, the Turkish version prevails. Türkçe metin

This notice explains, under Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data ("KVKK"), which personal data is processed through kalibremarkets.com (the "Site"), for what purposes and on what legal grounds, to whom it is transferred, and what your rights are.

1. Data controller

[COMPANY NAME] ("Kalibre Markets"). Address: [ADDRESS]. E-mail: markets@kalibrefin.com

Separate services offered at oyun.kalibremarkets.com and other subdomains of kalibremarkets.com are outside the scope of this notice.

2. No account or registration required

The Site's free tools are used without an account, and user accounts cannot be created on the Site. A Kalibre Lab subscription is likewise operated through a license key issued to you after payment, rather than through a user account (section 3(h)).

3. What data we process and why

a) When you visit the Site. Your IP address, browser and device information, the page requested and the time of the request are recorded in server logs by the Site's hosting provider. This information is processed to operate the Site, keep it secure and protect it against automated attacks. Legal ground: our legitimate interest in providing the Site securely (KVKK art. 5(2)(f)).

b) Visit statistics (Google Analytics). Used only if you select "Accept" in the cookie banner. In that case usage data such as a cookie identifier, the pages visited, visit duration, device and browser type and approximate location is processed to measure how the Site is used. Legal ground: your explicit consent (KVKK art. 5(1)). You may withdraw your consent at any time (see section 6).

c) AI assistant. Messages you write to the assistant are sent to Google's Gemini service, or to Groq if that service is unavailable, to generate a reply. Your messages are not stored on our servers; the last 20 messages of your chat history are kept only in your own browser. Do not write identity details, contact details or other personal data to the assistant. Legal ground: provision of the service you request (KVKK art. 5(2)(c)).

d) Subscription waitlist. The e-mail address you leave on the waitlist is sent to us through the FormSubmit service and is used only to inform you when Kalibre Lab subscriptions become available. Legal ground: the consent you give when signing up (KVKK art. 5(1)).

e) Contact e-mails. The links on the contact page open your e-mail program; your name, e-mail address and message are sent to us from your own e-mail account. This information is processed to assess your question or request and to reply to you. Legal ground: our legitimate interest in responding to your request (KVKK art. 5(2)(f)) and, where your request relates to entering into a contract, the establishment of that contract (KVKK art. 5(2)(c)).

f) Page translation. If you press the translation button on a page, the Google Translate service is loaded and the page content and your connection information are sent to Google for translation. The service is not loaded unless you press the button.

g) Fonts. The Site's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. In doing so your IP address and browser information are sent to Google's servers. Legal ground: our legitimate interest in displaying the Site (KVKK art. 5(2)(f)).

h) Subscription and license. When you subscribe, the payment page is provided by Stripe. Your name, e-mail address, card details and billing address are sent directly to Stripe; your card details do not reach us. The information we receive from Stripe and store is: your e-mail address, your Stripe customer and subscription numbers, the status of the subscription and its validity date. In addition we store the license key issued to you and the numbers of up to two MetaTrader 5 accounts on which the license has been used. When checking the license, Kalibre Engine sends the license key and the MT5 account number to our server; these requests may also appear in the hosting provider's server logs. To prevent abuse, a request counter per account number is kept for about two minutes. This information is used to run the subscription, bind the license to your account and keep payment records. Legal ground: the establishment and performance of a contract (KVKK art. 5(2)(c)) and, for invoice and tax records, compliance with a legal obligation (KVKK art. 5(2)(ç)).

4. MetaTrader 5 bridge

If you choose to connect Kalibre Lab to your MetaTrader 5 account, the Kalibre Bridge add-on that you install on your own computer reads the following information, encrypts it on your computer and transmits it through the Site's server only to your browser:

  • closed candles of the symbols you select: time, open, high, low and close price, tick volume;
  • your open positions: symbol, direction, lot size, opening time and price, current price, stop-loss and take-profit levels, profit or loss, and the number of the add-on that opened the position (magic number);
  • your account's currency, balance and equity;
  • the name of your broker, the name of the trading server and whether the account is a demo or a live account.

The add-on does not read your account number, password, identity details or trade history; it does not open trades and makes no changes to your account.

End-to-end encryption. This information is encrypted (AES-256) and sealed (HMAC-SHA256) on your computer with keys derived from the pairing code that Kalibre Lab generates in your browser. The pairing code exists only in your browser and in the add-on's settings; it is never transmitted to Kalibre Markets. Kalibre Markets therefore cannot access the content of the encrypted packets. Symbol names are also part of the encrypted content. Technical details are on the Security page.

What is processed on the server. For the bridge, the Site's server processes only: the encrypted packets; the size of the packets and the time they are received; a channel number derived from the pairing code that cannot be used to recover the code; and a counter kept to limit the number of writes per channel. The encrypted packets are held in a store provided by [STORAGE PROVIDER]; a packet is replaced when a new packet of the same type arrives and is in any case deleted automatically no later than two hours after it is received. Counters are deleted after about two minutes. The content of the packets is not written to server logs. During the connection your IP address is processed by the hosting provider to deliver the transmission (see 3(a)); Kalibre Markets does not store IP addresses in connection with the bridge.

Legal assessment. Because Kalibre Markets does not hold the key needed to decrypt the encrypted packets, it is debatable whether these packets constitute personal data from Kalibre Markets' perspective. For transparency, this processing is described here on the assumption that it is personal data. Legal ground: provision of the service you request (KVKK art. 5(2)(c)).

Using the bridge is optional. When you remove the add-on from the chart or close MetaTrader 5, data transmission stops; the last packets on the server are deleted within two hours at most.

5. Data that stays only in your browser

The following data is kept on your own device, in your browser's local storage, and is not sent to us:

  • your decision journal entries;
  • your strategy draft in Kalibre Lab;
  • the MetaTrader 5 bridge pairing code and, if you changed it, the relay address setting;
  • your bias test and chart test results and your pre-trade check history;
  • the last 20 messages of your chat with the assistant;
  • your onboarding tour and guide preferences and your cookie preference.

You can delete this data at any time using your browser's feature for clearing site data. Deleted data cannot be recovered. To invalidate a pairing code you can generate a new code in Kalibre Lab; after that, packets encrypted with the old code cannot be opened and you will need to enter the new code in the add-on.

6. Cookies

Cookies used on the Site:

  • Strictly necessary cookies. The cookie used by our hosting provider's security check. It is required for the Site to work and does not depend on your consent.
  • Analytics cookies. Google Analytics cookies (_ga and those beginning with _ga_, up to 2 years). Used only with your consent.

You can change your cookie preference at any time. When you withdraw your consent, the Google Analytics cookies are deleted from your browser.

7. Parties to whom data is transferred

Your personal data is transferred, limited to the purposes above, to the following service providers:

  • Vercel Inc. Hosting and security of the Site.
  • Google LLC. Google Analytics (only with your consent), Gemini (assistant), Google Translate (only if you press the translation button) and Google Fonts.
  • Groq, Inc. Fallback reply service for the assistant.
  • [STORAGE PROVIDER]. Storage of the MetaTrader 5 bridge's encrypted packets for up to two hours (the provider cannot read the content of the packets) and storage of subscription license records.
  • [STRIPE ENTITY] (Stripe). Collection of subscription payments, invoicing, subscription management and fraud prevention.
  • FormSubmit. Delivery of waitlist sign-ups.
  • Our e-mail service provider. Receipt and storage of e-mails you send us.

The servers of these service providers are located abroad, mainly in the United States of America; the servers of [STORAGE PROVIDER] may also be located abroad. The data described above is therefore transferred abroad.

Your personal data may also be transferred to public authorities and institutions authorised by law, in the cases provided for by law and upon request.

8. Retention periods

  • Server logs: for the limited period set by the hosting provider.
  • Google Analytics data: for the period set in the Google Analytics settings, at most 14 months.
  • Encrypted packets of the MetaTrader 5 bridge: at most two hours; write counters about two minutes.
  • Waitlist: until information about subscriptions has been sent or until you ask to be removed from the list.
  • License record (e-mail, Stripe numbers, license key, linked MT5 account numbers): for the duration of the subscription and at most one year after it ends. Payment and invoice records are kept at Stripe and in our accounting records for the period required by the applicable tax and commercial legislation.
  • Contact e-mails: after your request has been resolved, for the statutory limitation period for possible disputes.

Data whose retention period has expired is deleted, destroyed or anonymised.

9. Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK you have the right to:

  • learn whether your personal data is being processed and, if so, request information about it;
  • learn the purpose of the processing and whether the data is used in accordance with that purpose;
  • know the third parties in Turkey or abroad to whom it is transferred;
  • request correction if it has been processed incompletely or inaccurately;
  • request deletion or destruction under the conditions set out in the KVKK;
  • request that third parties to whom the data was transferred be notified of the correction, deletion or destruction;
  • object to a result to your detriment arising from analysis exclusively by automated systems;
  • claim compensation if you suffer damage because of unlawful processing.

10. How to make a request

To exercise your rights, you may send your request in writing to [ADDRESS], or e-mail it to markets@kalibrefin.com from an e-mail address you have previously provided to us and that is registered in our system. Your request should include information verifying your identity and a description of what you are asking for.

Your request will be resolved free of charge within thirty days at the latest. If the action requires an additional cost, a fee according to the tariff set by the Personal Data Protection Board may be charged. If your request is rejected, the answer is found insufficient or no answer is given in time, you may lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Board.

11. Children

The Site is not intended for persons under 18. If you are under 18, do not submit personal data to the Site.

12. Changes

This notice may be updated. The current text is published on this page together with its effective date. Updates that materially change how your personal data is processed are announced on this page before they are applied.
