Your license

Your subscription's license key appears here. You enter it once in the Kalibre add-ons; they renew it on their own while your subscription runs.

Do not share the key. It can be used on up to 2 MT5 accounts and is bound to the first accounts it is used on.

Download the add-ons

Both work with the same license key. Setup steps are on the setup page.

There is no license saved in this browser. If you have subscribed, paste your key below; otherwise see the pricing page.

Enter the key in Kalibre Engine

  1. Attach Kalibre Engine to a chart and open the Inputs tab.
  2. Paste your key into License key and click OK.
  3. Make sure MT5 allows WebRequest for https://kalibremarkets.com (step 4 of setup).

Engine checks the license a few seconds after starting and renews it every 6 hours. If the internet connection drops, it keeps working on the last valid license for 72 more hours. Historical tests in Strategy Tester need no license.

Cancelling and changing accounts

Manage subscription takes you to Stripe's secure page, where you can change your card, download invoices or cancel. If you cancel, your license stays valid until the end of the period.

To move your license to another MT5 account, write to us.
