If you trade your own account

The strongest driver of losses is leverage and position size. Fix risk first with a risk budget, volatility-scaled lots and a daily loss limit, then test the strategy.

For retail forex

Risk is asked about before return.

On a bank's treasury desk, a position is judged not by what it could earn but by what it could lose on its worst day. An economist, likewise, looks less at one period's result and more at whether the conditions behind it will repeat. Kalibre Lab puts those two questions at the start of every test.

Worst period first

The largest drawdown sits on the same line as the return. You size a strategy by what you could carry through its worst stretch.

Size from a risk budget

Position size comes not from a feeling but from the share of balance you risk, the stop distance and how volatile the market is right now.

Regimes change

A rule that works in a calm market may fail when volatility jumps. Volatility models show which conditions a strategy was tested in.

Setup takes three steps.

Your data leaves MetaTrader 5 and reaches the lab encrypted. The details, screen by screen, are on the setup page.

  1. Install the add-on

    Add the Kalibre Bridge add-on to MetaTrader 5. It places no trades; it only reads price history and your open positions.

  2. Paste the code, allow the address

    Paste the pairing code the lab creates into the add-on. In MT5's options, allow the Kalibre address once.

  3. Test your strategy

    Your own broker's candles arrive in the lab. Build your rule, run the quick test, then test the one you like in MT5.

What is in the lab?

A strategy is a rule file. It is built in the lab, rehearsed in the browser and run in MetaTrader 5 with the same file. Nothing is rewritten in between.

Rule file

Entry, exit, stop and target rules with SMA, EMA, RSI, ATR, ADX, Bollinger, MACD and volatility indicators. The rule you build is also written out as a plain sentence.

Quick test

Tries the rule on your own candles in seconds. It runs in the browser and leaves out costs; it is for ruling ideas out, not for confirming them.

Volatility lab

EWMA, Yang-Zhang and GARCH models. Shows when volatility clusters and which regime a strategy was tested in.

The final test in MetaTrader 5

The same rule file is tested in MT5 Strategy Tester with your broker's spread, commission and trading hours settings.

Live bridge

While the add-on runs, each newly closed candle and your open positions reach the lab encrypted. You can always see how fresh the connection is.

Your own file

You can also work without the add-on: just drag and drop a CSV or JSON file exported from MT5.

Your data. Your key.

Your prices leave your computer encrypted and are opened only in your browser. The key is the pairing code; we have no copy. Details are on the security page.

What our server sees

  • Unreadable, encrypted packets
  • Packet size and arrival time
  • A random-looking channel number

What our server cannot see

  • Which symbols you follow, and the prices
  • Your positions, balance, profit and loss
  • Your account number and password
  • Your pairing code, the key itself

Packets are deleted on their own after two hours at most.

What Kalibre Lab is not

  • Not investment advice. You decide which rule to build and when to trade which symbol.
  • It does not decide for you. The bridge add-on only reads. Kalibre Engine trades only with the rule file you built, on your account, when you start it.
  • It does not sell market data. Prices come from your broker, through your account. We publish no prices.
  • It does not promise returns. A rule that worked in the past may not work in the future. The lab exists to make that easier to see.
