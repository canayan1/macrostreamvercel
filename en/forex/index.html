Don't blow up your account.

Most retail leveraged trading accounts lose money. The strongest driver of those losses is leverage and position size; strategy comes second. Kalibre Lab fixes those two first.

Open the labRead the setup guide

Why risk first?

  • Loss rates are high. According to the European regulator ESMA's 2018 review, 74-89% of retail CFD accounts lost money. In Turkey, the Capital Markets Board said in 2015 that about 87-88% of forex investors lost money, and cut maximum leverage to 1:10 in 2017.
  • Leverage magnifies losses. An academic study of the 2010 US leverage cap (Heimer and Simsek, Journal of Financial Economics, 2019) found that the cap reduced high-leverage traders' losses by about 40%.
  • Volatility changes, so should your lot size. When volatility rises, the same lot means more risk. Scaling positions to volatility has been shown not to raise returns meaningfully in currencies, but to reduce extreme losses (Harvey et al., 2018). That is why our promise is not "win more" but "blow up less".

In Kalibre Lab

  • Risk budget: you decide what percentage of your balance each trade risks; the lot is calculated from the stop distance.
  • Volatility scaling: when volatility rises above normal, risk shrinks on its own (EWMA, Yang-Zhang, GARCH).
  • Daily loss limit and news filter: to stop on a bad day and to stay out around high-impact releases.
  • Your own data: you test your rule on your broker's prices; the final test is in MT5 Strategy Tester, with your own cost settings.

Your broker
