Why trading data needs an analytics layer

Every trade is recorded somewhere. Yet simple questions about those records still take a day of spreadsheet work.

  1. 01

    Blotters live in spreadsheets

    A dealing desk keeps its deals in an Excel blotter exported from another system. Reports are rebuilt by hand, and every rebuild can break.

  2. 02

    P&L that cannot be traced

    A daily figure gets reported, but the split between spot, swap points and carry is hard to show. When someone questions a number, finding the deals behind it is slow.

  3. 03

    Limits checked by hand

    Position and loss limits are often checked after the fact, by someone reading a sheet. A breach can be found late.

What we believe

Four principles decide what we build and what we leave out.

  1. 01

    Check the number before you show it

    Our engine is compared trade by trade against the MetaTrader 5 Strategy Tester. A separate reference implementation, written without access to the engine code, recomputes the same rules. Neither check left an unexplained difference. Read the methodology

  2. 02

    Data stays on your side

    Files are processed in your browser and are not uploaded. Live MT5 data is encrypted end to end with AES-256, and the relay deletes it within 2 hours. We never ask for a password. Read the security details

  3. 03

    Software, not a broker

    We do not give investment advice or trading signals. We do not hold client funds, and our servers do not send orders.

  4. 04

    Say what is ready

    Every product carries a status label: Beta, In testing, In development or Planned. We do not publish performance claims.
