Kalibre Markets

Kalibre Markets builds analytics software for trading data: for individual traders, prop traders and trading desks.

We are an early-stage fintech venture. Our products are in beta.

What we are building

  • Trade Analytics Beta: See where your trading makes and loses money.
  • FX Blotter Analytics In development: Realized P&L and positions from your desk's own blotter.
  • Rule Guard In testing: Prop firm loss limits, checked on every tick in MT5.
  • Kalibre Lab Beta: Build rules, backtest them and check settings for overfitting.
  • Kalibre Bridge for MT5 Beta: Encrypted, read-only link from MetaTrader 5.
  • CSV and Excel trade logs Beta: Bring trades from any platform or spreadsheet.
  • Alpaca and Tradier Beta: US brokerage accounts, read-only, with your own keys.

Principles

A verified engine

We check how a number is computed before we show it. The engine is compared against the MetaTrader 5 Strategy Tester and a separate reference implementation. Methodology

Local-first privacy

Files are processed in your browser. Live MT5 data is encrypted end to end. We never ask for passwords. Security

Software only

We do not provide investment advice, trading signals or copy trading. We do not hold client funds and do not send orders from our servers.

Honest status labels

Every product shows its status: Beta, In testing, In development or Planned. We make no performance claims.

Brokers and prop firms

We are working on analytics and rule monitoring across many accounts, and on trader dashboards under your own brand. This area is in discovery. To tell us what you need, email markets@kalibrefin.com.
