## 3. Majority positions applied in the drafts

1. **Entity and law.** One named entity; law and courts of [COUNTRY]; consumers keep their home-country mandatory protections and may sue at home. Follows FX Blue, Myfxbook, Edgewonk (home law), TradingView and 3Commas (consumer carve-out).
2. **No arbitration.** Follows FX Blue, Myfxbook, Edgewonk, TradeZella, TraderSync, 3Commas. TradingView and QuantConnect (US entities) are the exception; flagged for counsel.
3. **Liability cap.** Greater of 12 months' fees or USD 100, indirect loss excluded, non-excludable liability preserved. Follows QuantConnect (formula), FX Blue (fees-paid cap), Edgewonk and 3Commas (carve-outs).
4. **Warranties.** As is / as available, subject to law. All competitors.
5. **Indemnity.** Kept but narrowed to third-party claims from your breach, excluding our fault, consumers only as far as law allows. Follows Edgewonk and 3Commas; everyone has some indemnity.
6. **Withdrawal.** 14 days with express-consent waiver at checkout. Follows Edgewonk and 3Commas; it also matches the checkbox already in `api/odeme/baslat.ts`.
7. **Refunds.** No refund or pro-rata for a started monthly period, with listed exceptions. Follows Myfxbook, Edgewonk, TradeZella, TraderSync; exceptions follow Myfxbook (technical fault, erroneous order) and 3Commas (pro-rata on discontinuation).
8. **Renewal.** Automatic monthly renewal, cancel any time effective at period end, reminder before a free period converts, 30 days notice of price changes. Follows TradingView, Edgewonk, TraderSync, 3Commas.
9. **Language.** Competitors say nothing; we state English governs, because a Turkish version will also exist.
10. **Eligibility.** 18+, legal capacity, local-law responsibility, no sanctioned persons or territories (UN, EU, UK, US OFAC, [COUNTRY]). Follows FX Blue, Myfxbook, QuantConnect, 3Commas.
11. **US users.** Not excluded (no competitor excludes them); flagged for counsel because Kalibre Engine sends orders.
12. **Termination.** For cause without refund; without cause with notice and pro-rata refund; self-service deletion. Follows Edgewonk and 3Commas for the notice part, TradingView and QuantConnect for self-deletion.
13. **Marketing email.** Majority is opt-out/soft opt-in, but our code already records an unticked opt-in; drafts describe the code. Counsel item.
14. **Processors, retention, cookies.** Named processors (TradingView, Edgewonk), retention table (FX Blue, TradingView), separate cookie notice with opt-in for analytics (FX Blue, TradingView, Edgewonk).
15. **Hypothetical results.** CFTC Rule 4.41 statement plus our own limits. Follows Myfxbook and TraderSync.
16. **Not advice / not a broker / no custody.** Follows Myfxbook, QuantConnect, TraderSync, 3Commas.

## 4. Facts checked against our code (site-wt/api, site-wt/docs, kalibre-lab)

- Accounts: Supabase Auth, email one-time link or code and Google OAuth (`static/kalibre-auth.js`). Stored in Supabase: `profiles` (email, display name, locale, `marketing_consent` default false with timestamp, `terms_accepted_at`, `terms_version`), `user_items` (saved strategies, watchlists, settings, notes; synced only when signed in; max 200 items, 256 KB each), `licenses` view (hash id, last characters of key, status, valid until, add-ons, beta). No prices, positions, trade history, pairing code or broker keys (`docs/supabase-kurulum.sql`).
- Licence master record in Upstash Redis: key, email, Stripe customer and subscription IDs, status, paid-until time, up to 2 MT5 account numbers, creation time, product scope, Supabase user id (`api/_lib/lisans.ts`). Checkout-session pointer kept 7 days. Rate counters about 2 minutes.
- Licence check: Kalibre Engine **and Kalibre Bridge** both call `/api/lisans` with licence key and MT5 account number (`ACCOUNT_LOGIN`) at start and about every 6 hours (`mql5/Include/Kalibre/Lisans.mqh`, `KalibreKopru.mq5`). **Correction needed:** the current EN/TR privacy text says the Bridge "does not read your account number". It does not put it in relay packets, but it does send it for the licence check. The new privacy draft says this correctly.
- Relay: ciphertext only, TTL 2 hours, write limit 60 per minute per channel, no IP or body stored by our code (`api/kopru.ts`).
- Checkout: requires a signed-in account, terms acceptance and the withdrawal-consent checkbox; terms version, consent "yes" and consent timestamp are written to Stripe metadata; promotion codes allowed; card not requested for a 100% code until the first paid invoice (`api/odeme/baslat.ts`).
- Past-due grace: 3 days (`GECIKME_TOLERANSI_SN`). Licence bound to max 2 MT5 accounts. Downloads of add-ons blocked after expiry.
- Account deletion: sets paid subscriptions to cancel at period end, deletes the Supabase user (cascade), removes account links; the licence keeps working in MT5 until the paid period ends (`api/hesap.ts`).
- Strategy Tester and optimisation work without a licence; live trading with Engine needs a subscription (`kalibre-lab/docs/lisans.md`).
