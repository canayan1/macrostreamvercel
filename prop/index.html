Prop Shield: loss limits for prop firm challenges

Kalibre Engine checks your Maximum Daily Loss and Maximum Loss on every tick. It blocks new trades near the limit and closes its own positions at the safety margin.

Join the waitlistCheck your account

How Prop Shield works

  • Daily and total floors. The daily floor is based on the start-of-day balance. The total floor is based on the starting or peak balance. The higher floor applies. The day resets at the firm's time, which is 00:00 CE(S)T for FTMO.
  • Lot sizing. The loss of a new trade at its stop, plus the open risk to the stops of existing positions, cannot exceed the distance to the floor. Positions you opened manually are included.
  • Stop, then close. At twice the safety margin from the floor, no new trades are opened. At one safety margin, Kalibre Engine closes its own positions and opens no trades until the day resets.
  • News rule. If the firm restricts trading around high-impact news, no new trades are opened in that window.

Scope and limits

  • Prop Shield is part of Kalibre Engine. Kalibre Engine runs the rule file you build in Kalibre Lab on your account. Kalibre does not sell signals.
  • Kalibre is not a prop firm and is not affiliated with any prop firm. Firm names are used only to describe their rules.
  • Slippage and gaps can cause losses beyond the stop. The safety margin is there for this.
  • Prop Shield is in beta and is not a guarantee. Read the risk disclosure before using it on a live account.
