How it works

Three steps from a file to a decision you make yourself.

FIG 01

FIG 02

Analyze

FIG 03

Act

Set loss limits with Rule Guard, or test a change to your rules in Kalibre Lab before you trade it.

Where you make and lose money

The list ranks segments by their contribution to net P&L. Each row shows the trade count, win rate against your average and, when it differs, your average size. A segment needs at least 20 trades to appear.

Prop limits, day by day

Pick a firm preset or enter your own percentages. Each day shows both floors, the lowest closed balance and how close it came. A history report has no intraday equity, so this view measures closed trades only.

Your own views

Group by any field, choose a measure and a chart. Views are saved in your browser and open again on your next visit.

Your data stays on your device

The privacy model is part of the architecture, not a setting. Read the security details

Files are read in your browser

MT5 reports, CSV and Excel files are parsed on your device and are not uploaded. The account number and name in a report are not read.

Live MT5 data is encrypted end to end

Kalibre Bridge encrypts each packet with AES-256 before it leaves the terminal. The relay stores only ciphertext and deletes it within 2 hours.

No passwords

We never ask for your MT5 or broker password. Alpaca and Tradier keys stay in your browser.
