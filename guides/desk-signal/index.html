Desk Signal · Guide · 5 minutes

Run Desk Signal with your team

A live desk where the head sends colour signals to traders in one key, urgent signals ring until answered, and every signal is logged with the time it was seen and done.

Start a deskDownload this guide (PDF)

What you need

  • One computer for the head of desk, with Chrome or Edge.
  • One computer per trader. A phone works, but it must keep the tab open and the screen on.
  • A way to send a link: chat, email or your desk's usual channel.

Steps

  1. Start the desk

    The head opens kalibremarkets.com/lab/desk-live, types a desk name and clicks Start the desk. This browser becomes the head console. Keep using the same browser: the key that sends signals lives in it.

  2. Back up the head key

    Click Download a backup of the head key and keep the file. It moves the desk to another browser if you need to.

  3. Invite the traders

    Click Invite traders, then Copy link, and send the link to each trader.

  4. Traders join

    Each trader opens the link, types a name and a location, and clicks Join and turn on sound. They appear on the head's screen as they join.

  5. Turn on sound and the orb

    Each trader clicks Test sound once. In Chrome or Edge, Keep the orb on top keeps a small orb above every other window.

  6. Send a signal

    Pick a signal (1 to 9), then a trader, or press A for everyone. Urgent signals ring until the trader opens them. After 10 seconds the orb grows; after 20 seconds the trader's row turns red on the head's screen.

  7. Read the answers and the log

    Traders click Seen, then Done. Who has answered shows the average time to see and to finish. Download Excel saves the full log.

What you will see

  • A coloured orb on each trader's screen.
  • Who has seen and who has done each signal, to the second.
  • Average time to answer per trader.
  • A signal log as CSV or Excel.

Good to know

  • Signals carry a fixed meaning from your board, never free text and never orders.
  • Change the meanings with Edit signals before you start.
  • The head of desk sets limits, never orders. Rule Guard enforces them only in MT5 terminals where the trader linked it.
  • The relay keeps the last 5,000 events for 30 days. Download the log to keep it longer.

Optional: set the desk's risk

The head of desk sets limits once and rides them with faders: one per trader, and a master fader for the whole desk. A trader's fader scales their max lot and daily loss limit; the master scales everyone's.

  1. Set the base limits

    In Desk risk, type the limits for a trader at full risk: max lot, daily loss limit, max trades a day, stop after losses, a pause after a loss. Leave a box empty to keep that limit off.

  2. Ride the faders

    Move a trader's fader or the master. Each strip shows the lot it gives. Half risk and Defensive set the master in one click.

  3. Send the limits

    Click Send limits. Each trader whose limits changed sees New limits with the change, clicks Seen and Done, and the log records it. No new trades goes out at once.

  4. Keep them in MT5

    Traders link Rule Guard from their screen (see the Rule Guard guide). The Rule Guard column shows who has it on and the rule hits of the day.

  5. Use a controller

    In Chrome or Edge, plug in any MIDI fader box, DJ controller or pad grid and click Connect a controller, then Map controls: click a strip or a pad and move the control that should drive it. A fader takes over once it passes the level on screen.
