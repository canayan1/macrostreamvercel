Built to carry signals, and only signals.

Sealed on your computer.

Signals are encrypted before they leave the app, with a key that lives only in your invite link. Our relay passes them on without being able to open them.

Only the head sends.

Every signal carries the head of desk's digital signature. An invite link lets a trader answer, never send.

Nothing to run.

Apps exchange fixed signals from your board. No files, links or code travel between them.

One address on the internet.

The app talks only to kalibremarkets.com. It cannot open your files or start other programs, and it saves a log only where you choose.

Check what you downloaded.

Each file's SHA-256 is listed above. On a Mac run shasum -a 256 on the file; on Windows run Get-FileHash in PowerShell. The two values match.

Signed releases next.

Apple-notarized and code-signed builds replace this beta, and the system opens the app without asking first.
