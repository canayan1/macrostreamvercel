What it does.

Quicker than a chat message.

No window to find and nothing to type. The signal lights up the corner of every screen it is sent to.

Rings until answered.

Urgent signals keep sounding until the trader opens them. After 10 seconds the orb grows. After 20 seconds the trader's name turns red on your screen.

Every location on one board.

Send to a city with one key and see each trader's local time next to their answer.

A log for your records.

Sent, delivered, seen and done, to the second in UTC. Download it as CSV or Excel.

Fixed meanings, never orders.

A signal carries only a meaning from your board. No free text, no orders and no market data.

Set the desk's risk like a mixer.

Set the limits once, then ride a fader per trader and a master fader for the whole desk. Each trader sees the new limits and confirms them, and every change is in the log.

Your own controller.

Plug in any MIDI fader box, DJ controller or pad grid and map it in a minute. A fader takes over only once it passes the level on screen, so picking it up never jumps anyone's risk.

Kept in MT5 by Rule Guard.

Traders link Rule Guard with one pasted link. It applies the desk's limits in MetaTrader 5, and you see who has it on and which trades broke a limit.

Does it replace our chat or recorded lines?

No. It sits next to them for the quick signals. Your firm's own record-keeping rules still apply, and the signal log helps you keep them.

Does the demo send anything?

No. The demo runs in your browser and sends nothing to a server.

Can we choose our own colours and meanings?

Yes. Up to nine signals, each with its own meaning and key. You choose which ones ring until answered.

What does a trader need to install?

Nothing. They open the invite link in a browser tab and pick a name. In Chrome and Edge the orb can stay on top of their other windows. The desktop app follows.

Can the head of desk trade on my account?

No. The head sets limits only. Rule Guard, in your own MT5, never opens a trade; in Protect mode it closes a new position that breaks a limit, in Watch mode it only warns.

Can Kalibre read our signals?

No. Signals are sealed in your browsers with a key that lives only in your invite link. Our relay passes them on without being able to open them, and only the head's browser can send a signal.

How long is the log kept?

The head's console downloads the full log as CSV or Excel at any time. During early access the relay keeps the last 5,000 events for 30 days.
