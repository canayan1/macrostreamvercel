About Kalibre Markets

Kalibre Markets builds software for traders. Our first product, Kalibre Lab, is a backtesting and risk toolkit for MetaTrader 5, Alpaca and Tradier accounts.

Kalibre Lab

  • Strategy Builder and Quick Backtest: build entry and exit rules and test them on your data in the browser.
  • Optimization: scan parameters with separate training and test periods.
  • Dashboard: balance, positions, quotes and closed trades from your connected account.
  • Kalibre Bridge and Kalibre Engine: MT5 add-ons for encrypted data sync and for running your rule file.
  • Prop Shield: daily and total loss limits for prop firm challenges.

How we work

  • We publish no market data. The lab uses your account's data, your files or labelled SYNTHETIC samples.
  • Bridge data is end-to-end encrypted. Our servers cannot decrypt it (security).
  • The method is documented. We describe how each number is calculated and what it leaves out (methodology).
  • We do not sell signals or give advice. You choose the rules and the trades.
